## What the concept publishes

- Full street/locality/road/city/region/country address **without a PIN**; a visible note explains why.
- Phone/WhatsApp number.
- Rating and review count, with Google Business attribution and a link to the public review listing.
- Room types as prompts to ask about, with explicit “availability to confirm” and “current price on enquiry” messages.
- Amenities as an enquiry checklist, not as guaranteed inclusions.
- Representative images with prominent warnings that they are not Guardian PG property photos.
